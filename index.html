— THE PROJECT OVERVIEW —

Sattva Aangané is Sattva Group’s premier lakeside landmark — an elevated sanctuary where architecture, biophilic greenery, and stillness come together across ~10 acres with over 80% open landscaped spaces.

Inspired by the timeless Indian aangan (courtyard), the development features 5 iconic high-rise towers scaling up to 42 floors, overlooking the pristine waters of Cheemasandra Lake. Designed with expansive private balconies, cross-ventilation, and large-format 3 & 4 BHK homes and exclusive double-height duplexes.

Includes an opulent 45,000 sqft Grand Luxury Clubhouse, lakeside infinity pool, outdoor athletic courts, meditation gazebos, and serene nature trails. Perfect for discerning families seeking an elevated sanctuary and investors targeting top-tier capital appreciation in East Bengaluru.

Sattva Aangané, Budigere Cross,
Project Overview & Highlights

An iconic high-rise sanctuary comprising 5 towers soaring up to 42 storeys beside Cheemasandra Lake, crafted with Sattva’s benchmark construction quality, expansive courtyard greens, and high-speed transit via Old Madras Road (NH-75) and STRR.

A lakeside biophilic sanctuary: Over 80% of the ~10-acre enclave is dedicated to landscaped courtyards, reflexology trails, and water installations with zero surface vehicular movement.
5 Iconic towers rising up to 42 floors: Monolithic seismic-engineered towers featuring imported marble, 8-foot smart-lock main doors, deep viewing balconies, and high-speed elevators.
Large-format luxury homes: From 1,794 sq ft 3 BHK luxury residences to expansive 5,000+ sq ft Sky Duplexes, each designed according to Vastu with panoramic unobstructed lake views.
45,000 sq ft Grand Luxury Clubhouse: Lakeside infinity swimming pool, indoor heated pool, state-of-the-art gym, badminton, squash, luxury spa, and open-air amphitheatre.
Premier connectivity on Old Madras Road: Directly situated on NH-75 near Budigere Cross, 10 mins to Whitefield Station, 12 mins to Kadugodi Metro, 15 mins to ITPL, and 30 mins to Kempegowda Airport.
