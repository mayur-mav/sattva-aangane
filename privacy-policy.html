SATTVA AANGANÉ ENQUIRY PAGE · PUBLISHED BY M&A VENTURES, AUTHORISED CHANNEL PARTNER

This page explains what happens to the details you share through the enquiry forms, callback requests and WhatsApp links on the Sattva Aangané project page operated by M&A Ventures (Authorised Channel Partner for Sattva Group). This page is an informational channel partner property portal.

What we collect

  • Your name, mobile number and, if provided, your email address.
  • The unit configuration you are interested in (e.g. 3 BHK Luxury, 3 BHK Premium + Maid, 4 BHK Royal Suite, 4 BHK Signature + Maid, 5 BHK Duplex Sky Villa) and the form/button used to submit your enquiry.
  • Campaign attribution parameters (such as UTM source, medium, campaign tags) and referral sources.
  • Standard technical usage statistics collected through analytics tools, including browser type, operating system, and approximate geographical region.

Why we collect it

  • To provide requested project brochures, verified cost sheets, floor plans, and RERA disclosures for Sattva Aangané.
  • To schedule personalised guided site visits to the experience gallery on Old Madras Road near Budigere Cross.
  • To coordinate follow-ups with our senior property advisors and keep you informed of launch allocations and milestone developments.

Who receives it

Your enquiry information is securely maintained by M&A Ventures and its certified property advisors. It may be coordinated with the builder sales desk at Sattva Group for allotment verification, pricing confirmations, and booking documentation. We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third-party commercial databases.

Consent to be contacted

By submitting any enquiry or callback form on this page, you authorise M&A Ventures and its accredited sales representatives to contact you via telephone call, SMS, WhatsApp, and email regarding Sattva Aangané and relevant residential developments by Sattva Group, overriding any DND or NDNC registration.

Cookies and tracking

This website may store temporary cookies and attribution parameters for campaign performance measurement and session optimization. You can manage or disable cookie tracking through your browser settings at any time without impacting your ability to submit enquiries.

Retention and your rights

We retain enquiry records in accordance with regulatory requirements and advisory follow-up needs. You have the right to review, update, or request deletion of your stored contact details by contacting us at info@markanthony.in.
